## Welcome to NoteDown
Every note is also a to-do: the round button in front of it closes it, and the archive keeps it.

- [ ] Tap or click this text to edit it; the toolbar adds headings, lists and tasks
- [ ] Add a note with **+**, or hold **+** to dictate one
- [ ] Long-press a note (Ctrl+click on a computer) to select several and copy, merge or share them
- [ ] Turn on sync under ⋮ → **Sync & sharing** to have your notes on all your devices
- [ ] Close this note when you're done
