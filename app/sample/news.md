## Search shows its hits
Every hit is highlighted in the notes and in the contents. A counter shows how many there are, and ▲ / ▼ (or Enter and Shift+Enter) step through them.

## Verify your contacts
Contacts you add by scanning their code in person are verified. For one added by an invitation link, tap the contact and compare the safety code, e.g. on a call.

**Update:** you can now also scan the contact's code under "Verify contact".

## Due-date reminders
Turn them on under ⋮ → Reminders: on the due day, one notification lists the open notes due that day.
