Import from Keep

Open NoteDown
  1. Open Google Takeout, choose Deselect all, then tick only Keep.
  2. Export once as a .zip file and download it.
  3. Choose the ZIP here. You see every note first and pick the ones to take over.

Drop the Takeout ZIP here

The file is read in this browser and never uploaded. Imported notes go to the private notes of NoteDown in this browser; with sync on, they reach your other devices from there.

They are in your notes now, with their dates; archived ones are in the archive. Labels from Keep are #labels in the text.
